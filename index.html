No Man Sky FM for your FM-1

A free firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1: a space adventure you play with two knobs while the other hand makes the music. Fly over planets, race, mine, fight, warp between stars.

New in v0.9v0.9

Still being optimized. This version brings a lot at once, so the 3D flight and some screens can still lose frames, and more optimization is coming in the next versions. Keep a backup, and tell us what feels slow.

Choose a planet and you come down into it flying, behind the ship, over the same ground you walk on: seas, sand, grass, trees, hills and peaks in 3D.

KNOB 1turn KNOB 2speed OCT− heldboost OCT+ heldland on land: a tree under the ship blows to pixels, then you walk. Over water it refuses KNOB 3turned down: back to space
  • Over a planet: fly at top speed for a moment and four ships come up beside you. First through the six beams of light wins credits.
  • In that race, hold OCT+ over land to land for a moment while the others fly on. Hold it again to fly.
  • In space: a ship with a chequered flag shows up now and then, when you stand still or ahead of you. Hold OCT+ by it for the road race, then you are back in space.

Inspired by Galactic Racer, reduced to what fits on the FM-1. The race flags on planets, the gates at stations and the neon space race are gone: the room went to the new ones.

Every time a sound changes, the sounds show as CDs in a 3D carousel, with each one’s name on it: the new one rolls in, turns round and spins, and the screen goes back to where you were.

PRESETSon HOME, on the PRESETS page, in TRACKS KNOB 1on the PRESETS page drum kiton the drum track

From the tapes of winampFM: the same 42 colour sets and the same way of making the art, on discs.

  • SLOOP 2.8.4: more stable MIDI clock, resonance on every track filter, a filter on the drums, cleaner fades, UNISON and PHYS fixes, SAMPLE and GRAIN follow the top keys, a sturdier second core, more reliable rescue.
  • FX > PARTY (from winampFM): who is on stage. Solo, duel, trio or all, a lead, and the stage can turn by itself every 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars.
  • ARP 2 > ORD SCL (from winampFM): one key plays the whole scale, two keys the scale between them. With HOLD on, the same note again stops it.
  • EDIT > WAVE, a mini sample editor (after the ½ sampler): the wave on the whole screen with IN, OUT, LOOP and TUNE on the knobs.
  • Menu > SEQ: STEP ADVANCE, DRUM PREVIEW and FILTER RESO.
  • To make room, BASS, hats and cymbals went from 22 to 16 kHz and SCRATCH to 11 kHz. No sound was removed.
  • The No Man Sky FM editor: the SLOOP editor by isod89 with our name and colours. Songs, sounds, piano roll, MIDI and backups from the computer.
  • The whole simulator on this page: the 3D model, the screen pixel for pixel and the controls side by side.
  • Six languages: English, Portuguese, Korean, Japanese, German and French.

All the music in the video was made live on the FM-1, while playing the game. Filmed on an earlier version.

A quick press keeps the usual function. Only holding, double tapping or pressing together does something new: tapped once, OCT− and OCT+ still change the octave, and every other button does what it always did on SLOOP.

Every version

What came in each one.

v0.9 · The planet in 3D, new races, CDs, SLOOP 2.8.4. Released.

  • Flight over each planet in 3D, on the ground you walk on; landing on land, trees that blow to pixels
  • Race over the planet with four rivals and six beams; a ship with a flag starts the road race in space
  • CDs on every sound change (after the tapes of winampFM)
  • Merged SLOOP 2.8.4 by isod89
  • PARTY and the scale arpeggio (from winampFM)
  • Screens over the game much lighter: only what changes goes to the LCD
  • The editor on the site, the whole simulator, six languages
  • Removed to make room: race flags on planets, station gates, the neon space race. Lower sample rates on BASS, hats, cymbals and SCRATCH

v0.6 · Test builds, never released.

  • EDIT > WAVE, the mini sample editor (after the ½ sampler)
  • A strip that says what the four knobs do, for two seconds after a turn
  • Dots that show the page you are on
  • Colours of each track in the visuals

v0.5 · The galaxy got busier.

  • Battles out in space: a boss that fires bursts and goes down in 30 s, little ships that keep coming
  • Pirates raid the system now and then: 2 to 6 ships
  • Hold keys to call escorts: little white ships with long trails
  • Races on planets and on a neon road in space, with gears and a pixel dashboard
  • A hangar in every station: five ships, engine and weapon upgrades
  • People on some planets with quests, ships passing by that talk to you
  • REC keeps the arp and the punch-in FX, saved with the project
  • A new take-off and close-up drawn from a 3D model of the ship

v0.4 · First public version: site, installer, simulator.

  • Merged SLOOP 2.7.1: the sound on both cores, 15 arp modes, KBD HOLD, MIDI 3, SAVE SECTION A–D, favourite sounds, REC QUANT off, looping user samples, knob acceleration, USB audio rate
  • KEY LED: the light a key makes (from winampFM)
  • GLO > DRUM SOUND: a drum’s TUNE and DECAY
  • The opening with its own music; a nebula behind the stars; rings around planets

v0.3 · A galaxy to travel.

  • Galactic map, warp, stations, trade, fuel and saved games
  • Sentinels on planets, laser combat, hostile planets, pursuit into space
  • Boost, pulse jump, auto boost, radar, close-up, cabin
  • Visuals behind the knobs you turn; take-off and warp films

v0.2 · The game on HOME.

  • A space game on HOME, inspired by Low Mem Sky (PICO-8) and No Man’s Sky: stars, globe planets, landing, walking, resources, creatures, scanner
  • Four colours over black and white; pixel textures instead of inverted text

v0.1 · The base.

  • Built on SLOOP 2.6 by isod89 (based on Felucca by Leo Kuroshita): the same sound and buttons
  • New screens: black and white, numbers and bars only

No Man Sky FM is based on SLOOP 2.8.4 by isod89, which is based on Felucca by Leo Kuroshita (Hügelton Instruments). GPL-3.0. Tapes, PARTY, the scale arpeggio and KEY LED come from winampFM; the sample editor follows the ½ sampler; the game is inspired by Low Mem Sky (PICO-8).

    
The source of the whole firmware is open: use the features in your own work, just credit No Man Sky FM in your README or video. GitHub
